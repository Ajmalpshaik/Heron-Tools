# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be respectful, be helpful, assume good intent. People here are modellers, coordinators and developers with
very different backgrounds - explain, don't talk down.

## Our standards

Welcome:

- Clear, kind and specific feedback on the work, not the person.
- Asking questions - there is no stupid question about Revit or code.
- Crediting others' ideas and work.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour.
- Publishing someone's private information, or a client's project data, without permission.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to anyone representing the
project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@Ajmalpshaik](https://github.com/Ajmalpshaik).
Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close discussions, or block
contributors who do not follow this code.
